Terms & Fair Play

  • Users must be 18+ and responsible for maintaining the security of their account credentials.
  • Promo codes are redeemable once per unique user account and may have a global redemption cap or expiration set by administrators.
  • Arcade games (Aviator, Dragon Tiger, Andar Bahar, Mines) carry risk; only play with amounts you are comfortable wagering.
  • Abuse, multi-accounting, or automated exploitation may result in account review or suspension.

Privacy Policy

We store account profile identifiers, wallet balances, node holdings, transaction logs, and in-app notifications necessary to operate your account. We do not sell personal contact details to third-party data brokers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did a promo code say "already redeemed by your account"?

Promo codes are configured as one-time-per-user so each player claims their bonus once while other eligible users can still use an active code.

How do I earn VIP Milestone Spins?

Every 5,000 PKR in total arcade bets grants 1 VIP spin ticket (up to Rs. 50,000 prizes).
